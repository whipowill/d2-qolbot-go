# Team sync

The user's principle (2026-11-14): bot-led runs are **one comprehensive team sync: always together, always protected, always safe** ("these guys are
embarking on a combat mission against the forces of Hell and Satan himself: safety is key"). This file is the specification (the rules, as the user gave
them), the map of the code that implements each rule, and how to test it.

**Scope: bot-led runs only, in every mode (quest, farm, waypoint sync, level hunt, gold hunt).** When a real person is the leader (you play and the bots
follow you) none of this applies: a bot that needs town goes alone and comes back by its own portal, nobody waits for anybody. The code is gated by
`Team.isLeader()` (a bot with an objective and nobody to follow), `Team.syncOn()` (the leader, or a bot whose plan has `partySync`; off while you play) and
`firstAct` being 0 while you play. `scripts/jstest/team_test.js` has a test that none of it fires when a human leads.

## 1. The rules (the user's words)

> **"bots always stick together, with maybe a 12 second grace period. if anyone goes to town they all go to town. if anyone goes back to the field
> they all go back to the field."** ("if we get the party sync right, the bots never die and they crush the world", 2026-11-15)

> **"the entire team should always be together in the field. always. no exceptions. if it's a quest, a waypoint hunt, a level hunt, a farm, a gold hunt
> -- they are always together. if one person is missing a waypoint in a waypoint hunt and wanders into the field by themselves bc they are the only
> person who didn't have that waypoint -- then we have failed to obey the rules."** (2026-11-15)

## 2. Where each rule lives (all in d2bs/kolbot)

| Rule | Mechanism | Files |
|---|---|---|
| R1 | Every way into town from the field goes through `PartySync.call`: `Town.goToTown` (`Town.teamFollows`, under every one of its ~200 callers), `Pather.useWaypoint` (a wrapper at the end of Pather.js) and `Town.visitTown` (heal and chores trips). Not when the bot only follows its leader who is already in town, or another bot's trip is on. The leader's call is `data/partycall.json`; a follower's is a report to the leader instead (R10). Every mate's TownChicken thread checks for a call every 1 s and answers whatever it is doing (no busy, no monster check). The walk to the portal the call names (`portal`, `Pather.preferPortalOf`; any party portal close by if that one is not) runs with `Pather.fleeing`: no `NodeAction` (path clearing, chests, shrines, pick-ups); a blocked step still fights. | Town.js, Pather.js, PartySync.js, tools/TownChicken.js |
| R2 | **The town gate**, `Team.waitToLeaveTown(targetArea)`, under `Pather.moveToExit`, `useWaypoint` and `usePortal` when in town, for the LEADER only (R10: a follower goes when the leader tells it to: at the same three places `Team.mayLeaveTown(targetArea)` lets a follower in town go toward the field only when the leader's newest order is `out` or an errand to it, else the way out returns false and it logs "the leader has not sent the team out"; another town is free; its readiness is `Team.markReady`, said every 4 s while it is idle in town, Follower.js): into the field only when every mate in the game is in this town and says ready (`ready`, `leaving` or `proceed`, `Team.readyHere`), or is already out where I am going. Town to town: inside a chore trip (`Team.chore`, set by `Town.doChores` and `MercHire.maybe`) it is not gated; otherwise (an act change: `Pather.useWaypoint` into another act's town, `ActTravel.change`'s NPC route) it is gated like any departure (`Team.waitToLeaveTown`, `actChange`): every mate here and ready, none left in the field or on its way from another town. No time limit while everybody is in town; a mate out in the field is called to town every timer. The 12 s grace (`limits.grace`) covers the retries of one departure, in the town it was given in. A trip ends with `PartySync.barrier`: the bot says ready; the leader goes back out the way it came; every other bot stays and follows the leader (`stayInTown`). | Team.js, Pather.js, PartySync.js, Town.js |
| R3 | `Team.sendOutFirst` under `Pather.usePortal`, for every bot that takes its own portal from town (`Pather.getPortal(area, me.name)`): it waits until no mate is in town (a mate that says `proceed` itself is another maker at its own portal and does not count), then goes. No limit. Only a leader that made the portal says `proceed` (and again every timer); a follower that made it (a Sorceress's Quick Travel portal) says nothing: the leader's `proceed` naming it sent the team out (R10). | Team.js, Pather.js |
| R4 | The leader **names the portal** it goes through: its own (`sendOutFirst`), or somebody else's (`Pather.usePortal`: `proceed` with `owner` and area: a Sorceress's Quick Travel portal, a waypoint helper's, a mate's trip portal), or the one it just opened at a waypoint (`Waypoints.sendTeam`). A leader that rides a waypoint from town into the field while a mate lacks that waypoint opens its portal there at once and names it (the `Pather.useWaypoint` wrapper), so nobody has to ask for one (a walking waypoint fetch's first leg, a quest). A follower in town takes the named portal first, whether the leader is still in town or just went (`Follower`, `Team.returnCall`). The leader announces a waypoint or exit departure (`Team.announceLeaving(area, by)`); after a waypoint departure a follower never walks a town exit: the leader's portal, the waypoint if it has it, or it waits in town and asks for a portal. All of a follower's moves after the leader (exit, portal, waypoint) run without clearing (`Follower.flee`). | Pather.js, Team.js, Waypoints.js, bots/Follower.js |
| R5 | `Team.gather` fights back (`Attack.clear(15)`) while it waits with a fight in sight; the arrival wait on the far side of an exit (`Team.keepClose`) stops for a fight and asks again after it. The leader steps aside off the entrance tile (`stepAside`, `makeRoom`). | Team.js |
| R6 | `Team.limits.sync` = Config.RegroupSeconds (Settings > Behavior > Auto Regroup) x 1000; `gather`, `boss`, `far`, `depart` and `sendOut` all read it. After it, a split party: `Team.escalate` = in the field `Team.regroup()` (everybody to town, the town gate, out together), in town a call to town; reported in the Activity list as **"Sync broken: <why> in <area> after N s (<who, where>). Party regroup in town."** (`Report.problem`). No cap on regroups, no "on its way" extension, no give-up. Also TownChicken's own regroup for a mate apart for the same number of seconds (`Team.lost`). | Team.js, tools/TownChicken.js |
| R7 | `Team.roster()`: the bots of `data/bots.json` with their area; one with no area is not in the game and is not waited for. `PartySync.hold` (the leader waiting in town up to 3 min for a bot out of the game) is switched off. | Team.js, tools/TownChicken.js |
| R8 | `Waypoints.share` / `fetch`: the leader goes with the team, opens its portal at the waypoint and names it (`Waypoints.sendTeam`), and `Waypoints.waitForTeam` gathers the WHOLE team there (`Team.gather`) before the ones that lack it are waited for and everybody goes back. | Waypoints.js |
| R9 | `Attack.gatherBeforeBoss` (hold, wait, go), `Team.holdAndGather` in Quick Travel and quest scripts, `Quester.arrivedInAct` -> `waitForTeamInTown`; each bot picks up its own quest items (`QuestItems.shared`). | Attack.js, Quester.js, QuestScripts.js, QuickTravel.js |
| R10 | **The order record** `data/order.json`, written by the leader only (`Team.order`, read with `Team.currentOrder`): `team` = the newest order to everybody (`out` {area, by: exit / waypoint / portal / walk, owner}, `town` {area, portal, callId, urgent}, `stay`), `errand` = an order to one bot {to, place} (it counts for that bot when it is newer than the team order). `n` counts up; an order of another game (`me.gamename`) is ignored; nothing expires by time. Writers: `Team.announceLeaving` (out by exit/waypoint), every leader `proceed` (`Team.say` turns it into out by portal; `Pather.usePortal` says it for every portal the leader takes out of town, its own too), `PartySync.call` (town), `Team.setSolo` (stay), `QuickTravel.portal` and the waypoint helper (errand); and TownChicken records `out` (by walk) if the leader is out of town while the order is still `town`. Readers: `Team.leavingCall` (out by exit/waypoint), `Team.returnCall` (out by portal), `PartySync.townOrder` (town: TownChicken takes a follower in the field to town, once per order, counted only once it is in town, so a bot that was in town or on a waypoint when the order came still goes), and the Follower in town (with the newest order `town` or `stay` it does not leave town toward the field). A bot's `PartySync.session` is the game's start time (`me.gamestarttime`), the same in all its threads, and a bot never answers its own call. Quick Travel (`QuickTravel.portal`): the leader asks the Sorceress (or a waypoint helper) for a portal and waits in town with no time limit until she answers (`portalopen`, `noget`, `noquick`, `noplace`) or leaves the game; it logs "still waiting in town for X's portal" every 30 s. Only an answer that is not `portalopen` sends it on foot. The orders are: the call to town (`PartySync.call`), `proceed` (`sendOutFirst`, `Pather.usePortal` naming a portal, `Waypoints.sendTeam`, the `useWaypoint` wrapper), `leaving` (`Team.announceLeaving`), the regroup and the escalation (`Team.regroup`, `Team.escalate`), the commands (`Team.command`). Each is said by the leader only (`Team.isLeader()`, `PartySync.isLeader()`). A follower's `PartySync.call` writes `data/<profile>.wenttown.json` (where it came from, urgent or not) and waits in town like a caller did (`currentId`: the barrier marks it ready and hands it to the Follower). The leader's TownChicken thread reads the reports every 1 s while it is in the field (`PartySync.wentTown`), calls the team with that mate's name as the portal (`PartySync.call(area, urgent, name)`) and goes to town through it itself. **Goals erased in town**: TownChicken's `togglePause` notes whether it paused the main script in the field; when it resumes it in town it says `droptask` (`scriptBroadcast`). Follower.js sets `dropTask`; then `delay()` (wrapped) and `Pather.interrupt` throw, which unwinds everything the main script was doing up to its main loop, whose pass runs in a try: the next pass starts clean and autoFollow does what the newest order says. **Ready means ready**: a follower's "ready" comes only from its main loop idle in town (`Team.markReady`); the town thread says "chores" when a follower's trip starts and at its end (`PartySync.barrier`). **The maker obeys the last order**: a follower waiting to go last through its own portal (`Team.sendOutFirst`) stops when the newest order is no longer "out" and does not go out. | PartySync.js, Team.js, Pather.js, Town.js, tools/TownChicken.js |

**A Sorceress leader's own Quick Travel** (`QuickTravel.selfTrip`): the team waits in town at the portal spot (`Team.setSolo`); she teleports alone
(`Team.solo`: no gates on her way), opens her portal, comes back through it, and takes it out again as its maker: the town gate (everybody ready), then
`sendOutFirst` (she says `proceed`, the followers take her portal, she goes last), then `holdAndGather` at the place.

**The walking pace rule** (`Team.keepClose`: the leader stops up to 20 s for a mate more than 40 units behind, then not again for 60 s) is unchanged: a
pace rule inside one area, not a transition.

**The bots' keyword chat** (`data/<bot>.tsay.json`, one word per bot, `Team.say` / `heard` / `heardLately`): `ready` (done in town), `chores` (busy in
town), `chickentown` (going to town, come), `proceed` (area, owner: go out through that portal now), `idle`. The orders themselves are read from the
order record (R10), not from these words. Commands by the message log (`data/<bot>.tmsg.json`, `Team.post` / `command` / `take`): `hold`, `go`, Quick Travel requests.

## 4. What to look for in a live run (the log lines)

- `Party: X is going to town: calling the team.` (the leader) and `Party: X called the team to town; I am here.` (R1)
- `Party: X went to town: telling the leader.` (a follower), then the leader's `Party: X went to town: the leader calls the team to town through X's portal.` (R10)
- `Team: waiting in town until X are ready: we leave together.` (R2)
- `Team: I made this portal, so I go last: sending X out first.` (the leader) or `... waiting for X to go out first.` (a follower) / `Team: everybody is back out through my portal` (R3)
- `Follower: The leader sends the team out through X's portal.` (R4)
- `Party: the leader's order: to town through X's portal.` (a follower carrying out "town"), `Follower: the leader's last order is "town": waiting in town.`,
  `Team: out of town with the order still "town": the order is now "out" ...` (the leader's safety net; it should be rare) (R10)
- `Follower: back in town from a trip: what I was doing in the field is dropped.` and `Team: the leader has not sent the team out (last order: X): I stay in town.` (R10)
- `Team: waiting at the exit for X to come through.` / `Team: waiting for X to catch up` (R5, R9)
- **`Sync broken: ...`** in the Activity list (R6): every one is a bug. Look at what the late bots were doing (`not ready`, or clearing on the way).

## 5. Testing

`./scripts/jstest.sh` runs `scripts/jstest/*_test.js` with `gjs` (SpiderMonkey) against `harness.js`, a fake D2BS world (`me`, `getParty`, `delay` with a
fake clock, files, monsters). team_test covers the gate (the leader only, no time limit, a mate already out, town to town, ready at the gate), gather and the
far side of an exit (the one timer, regroups without a cap, no give-up), send-out-first (no limit, two makers), the waypoint share (the whole team, the
named portal), `announceLeaving`, the human-leader test, and R10 (a follower at its gate calls nobody; a follower that made a portal says no `proceed`);
partysync_test covers the call (urgent, not twice within 15 s; a follower's report; the leader's call naming the mate's portal), `wentTown`, pending,
`townOrder` (once per order; a bot in town when the order came still goes; a newer order replaces it), the session, and the trip's end. team_test also
covers the order record (leader only, n counts up, an errand for one bot, a new game, the last order trumps "leaving" and "proceed") and `mayLeaveTown` (a follower in town goes only on `out` or its errand).
`Pather` and `Follower` are not loaded in the simulation (the portal hand-off and the follower's choice of way are checked live only). It does not run
Diablo II: a live run is the final check, judged from the bots' logs and the "Sync broken" entries.
