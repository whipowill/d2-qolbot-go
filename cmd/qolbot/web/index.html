What This Is

Qolbot runs Diablo II characters as helpers. Either you play and the bots follow you into your game, or you step back and a leader bot hosts while the others follow, farming or playing quests.

Where It Comes From

The brains are Kolbot, the long-running Diablo II bot scripts by Kolton and its community, working inside D2BS, the library that lets scripts see and control the game. Qolbot launches them.

An Older Build

This is built on an older Kolbot and D2BS made for Diablo II 1.13, the final classic patch that PlugY runs on. Newer versions target other builds, so fixes and features were added here instead.

What Runs Where

On Linux and macOS the games run under Wine in a private prefix, hidden on Linux. They launch from your own game folder and Qolbot never changes your files. The logs are kept in its logs folder.

AutoEquip

Puts on better gear for a bot and its mercenary, in town and every half minute while a follower waits for you. It never touches items on your protected list, whatever their quality may be.

AutoStat

Spends a bot's stat points on its build as it levels, so no character is ever left holding points to spare. It can be switched off under Settings, Advancement, with the other two automations.

AutoSkill

Spends a bot's skill points in the order its build calls for, so each level adds to the right skill at once. It follows the build template, or the skills the character already favors the most.

Quest Mode

Quest Mode plays the quests act by act and picks the difficulty and the leader itself: the bot furthest behind leads. The others follow and claim rewards, and each finished quest is saved.

Protected Kinds

Under Settings, Inventory, you choose the kinds of items (uniques, sets and so on) that bots must never put on, replace, identify or sell. They stay exactly as you left them, wherever they are.

Your Pack

You need not set aside a working area. A bot never touches tomes, keys or the Horadric Cube, and anything else left in its pack counts as its own: stashed, or sold if junk, unless protected.

Your Charms

A bot knows which charms it picked up itself and which were already there. A charm you move, or take out of the stash again, becomes yours, and the bot never sells or stashes it afterwards.

Quest Rewards

Bots take and use the items that give stat points, skill points or life, and make room for them first. They never throw away a quest item, even when the pack is full of things they must keep.

They Follow You

When you or the leader leave town, change area, take a waypoint or a portal, the bots follow by exit, waypoint or portal, and they come back to town when you do, always staying close by.

They Stay Together

When a teammate is lost (not in my area and not in a town for the time set in Behavior, Party), the leader goes to town, everyone gathers there, and the team goes back out together again.

They Never Go Alone

A bot that cannot reach you waits in town and asks for a portal. It does not walk out looking for you, so a lone bot is never left wandering a dangerous place on its own, far from any help.

The Leader Waits

A leader bot starts a run only when the team it started is in the game, so nobody is left behind at the first area. It waits a few minutes, then goes without the ones who never showed up.

Quick Travel

With it on, a Sorceress in the party may place a portal at a boss so the team does not walk. She only places the portal and comes straight back, and then the whole team takes it to the boss.

They Save Often

Bots save every few minutes with the PlugY /save command, at once when a quest is finished or a level gained, and when they are stopped. Without PlugY only the save on stopping still works.

One Team

When bots run without you (a bot leads), in every mode, the team is one unit: it moves as one, goes to town as one, leaves town as one, goes on hunts as one, enters a new act as one and fights every boss as one. Always together, always protected, always safe. When you are the leader none of this applies: a bot that needs town goes alone and comes back by its own portal.

Nobody Strays

The leader never walks on while a mate is far behind: it stops for a mate more than 40 units away, for up to 20 seconds. It does not wait again for a minute for a mate that is stuck, so one stuck bot cannot hold the team for ever.

Anyone To Town, Everyone To Town

If any bot goes to town, for any reason (a heal, a repair, a full pack, a life chicken in a fight), the whole team takes the portal to town. A fight nearby does not stop them. Only a quest step in the middle of a chest or a weapon swap does, and then only for a short time.

The Maker Of The Portal Goes Last

The first bot back in town is the one whose portal brought everybody, and the portal closes when it takes it. So it goes back through last, after every mate has gone out. In town the bots say simple words to each other (ready, chores, chickentown, proceed): when everybody is ready the maker says proceed, the mates go, and the maker follows.

Leaving Town Together

Before the leader leaves town by waypoint, portal or exit, for a quest, a hunt, a farm or anything else, it asks: is the team ready, are we leaving together? Every mate must be in town and say it is ready.

Exits And Fights

At an exit the leader waits on the far side until every mate has come through. The one exception is a fight: a team that is fighting near an exit, with a mate one step on the other side of it, simply finishes the fight and is together again in the same zone afterwards. Nobody counts as lost during a fight.

Bosses And New Acts

Before a boss fight the leader says hold, waits until everybody is in the area, and says go, so that the whole team is there when the boss dies and the quest counts for all of them. A new act is entered together too, and the red portal to Act 4 is taken by everybody.

Nothing Just Expires

If the team is not together after a wait, the answer is not to go on without them: it is the regroup. Everybody goes to town, using a scroll if they have to, and the team goes back out together. If everybody is safe in town and one bot is only slow, the leader keeps waiting, up to five minutes. After three regroups in ten minutes the wait is given up and logged, so it cannot loop for ever.

Safety First

Bots drink potions and leave a fight by town portal when their life is low. A leader that has chickened often is not chosen to lead again soon, and a leader needs gold for repairs and potions, so it asks the team for gold before it runs short. A Sorceress that only follows walks with the team and uses Teleport only to place portals or to get out of trouble. Hunts follow the same rules, with zones fixed by you.

Where The Rules Live

The rules are in docs/TEAM_SYNC.md in the Qolbot folder, with a list of what each one does in the code. They are checked by a simulated game, so a change to the sync can be tested without starting Diablo II.

Missing points

Worked out from the character's save file, once, when the page first loaded (Rescan characters checks again). Checked: skill points (one per level after the first, plus Den of Evil, Radament and Fallen Angel), stat points (the class's start, five per level, plus Lam Esen's Tome) and life (level, Vitality and Alkor's Potion of Life). A save cannot show the Scroll of Resistance or the choose-an-item rewards (Charsi, Larzuk, Anya).
